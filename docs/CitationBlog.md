# The Citation Service for ESGF NG - CMIP7

The CMIP7 Citation service (which has since been expanded to accommodate CORDEX and CMIP6plus records) is a bespoke service to maintain CMIP7 DOI landing pages as part of the ESGF Next Generation project.

CMIP7 is the seventh phase of the Coupled Model Intercomparison Project, organised and funded by the World Climate Research Programme (WCRP). This latest iteration has specific focuses to address four Fundamental Research Questions using the data from various climate models at global and regional scales. 

## Remit of the Citation Service

The service was commissioned by the European Space Agency as a bespoke service to maintain and support citation of CMIP7 datasets for use in academic publications, such that data and responsible parties are properly credited for their contributions to the results of CMIP7 data analyses. These can be anything from independent research projects using CMIP7 data in the future, to the data and figures presented in the next Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change (IPCC) Assessment Report.
